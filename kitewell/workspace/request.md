Hi! I'm Jonas, I'm the product manager for onboarding at Kitewell. Customers who book on our site land in an empty workspace, and a third of them still have no project a week later. I want to change what happens around the booking without waiting for an engineering sprint each time. Engineering has given product two pages in the booking flow: configure, before the terms, and set up, after the booking. Our platform team says we can build them ourselves as an app with appos, using the booking functions engineering gives us (`kitewell:booking`). Here's what I need.

**The flow**: packages (sales) → configure (ours) → terms (sales) → review and book (engineering) → set up (ours). Each page sends the customer on; the booking functions say where.

**Configure.**

- The AI planning assistant, on or off, with its price per user per month from the catalog. On Business it's included: say "Included", no switch.
- A starter template for the first project, as cards with name and description from the catalog: Plan a quarter, Agency capacity plan, Product launch, or "Start empty". Preselect Agency capacity plan for the Agency package and Plan a quarter for everything else. Most people never change it, so the default matters.
- The current price at the bottom, as the booking functions calculate it, so turning the assistant on shows what it costs.
- One button: Continue.

**Set up**, right after the booking. The workspace is there the moment the customer books.

- "Your workspace is ready", its address, and the first project made from the template.
- "Who else plans with you?" Name and email, add one person at a time. Each person gets an invite link on the page with a Copy button. We send no email from here: the customer passes the links on.
- If someone can't be added (all seats taken, an email that isn't one), say why, as the booking functions give it.

**Why this matters to me:** I want to try things here every week, like another default template, or asking about the team before the templates. The pages are ours, so we can.

**Look:** the same as the sales pages, and it has to work on a phone.
